# Dataset License

The IMDB **dataset** is hosted externally and is licensed as described below.
(The code in this repository is separate and is covered by the MIT `LICENSE`.)

## Dataset licensing

- **IBIA (Indian Biological Images Archive) — primary repository, open download.**
  The dataset is released under the IBIA "Open Access" licence, governed by the
  Department of Biotechnology (DBT) BIOTECH-PRIDE guidelines and FeED protocols.
  As confirmed in writing by the IBIA/IBDC repository, this Open Access category
  is comparable to CC BY (reuse and redistribution with attribution). It is
  downloadable without registration or additional access controls.
  Study accessions: MAMOS_1000000004 (IMDB r1.0), MAMOS_1000000050 (IMDB r2.0).

- **MIDAS (Medical Imaging Data Analysis System) — access on request.**
  The dataset is also hosted on MIDAS under an explicit
  **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** licence
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Access on MIDAS is granted on
  request.
